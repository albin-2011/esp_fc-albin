🎮 ESP-NOW Radio Transmitter (TX) Builder — Step-by-Step from Build to Fly

Turn a second ESP32, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3, or ESP8266 + 2 Analog Joysticks into a 100Hz Low-Latency Drone Remote Controller.

STEP 1 Parts & Wiring

• 1x ESP32 / S3 / C3 / ESP8266
• 2x Dual-Axis Analog Joysticks (KY-023 or gimbal pots)
• 2x Toggle Switches (ARM & Flight Mode)
• CRITICAL: Power joysticks from 3.3V (never 5V) and wire axes to ADC1 pins because Wi-Fi uses ADC2!

STEP 2 Flash TX Code

• Select your Transmitter ESP board below.
• Copy the auto-generated ESP_FC_TX.ino sketch and upload it to your 2nd ESP.
• Keep the right stick (Roll/Pitch) untouched during boot so it auto-calibrates center (`1500 µs`).

STEP 3 Bind & Verify Bars

• Both Drone and Transmitter lock onto Wi-Fi Channel 1 using ESP-NOW Broadcast (`FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF`).
• Connect the drone to this Configurator, open the Receiver Tab, and move your sticks to verify `1000–2000 µs` bars!

STEP 4 Pre-Flight & Fly

• Props OFF test first! Check motor spin directions (`M1/M4 CCW`, `M2/M3 CW`).
• Run the 6-Point Accel Calibration Wizard in Setup.
• Mount props, lower throttle to `1000`, flip the ARM switch, and gently lift off!
